## 1. Risk Score Audit & Formula Specification

The backend risk score is calculated in `src/scoring.py` using a transparent, explainable formula:

$$\text{Risk Score} = 0.50 \times \text{Rule Score} + 0.30 \times \text{ML Anomaly Percentile} + 0.20 \times \text{Statistical Risk Score}$$

### Formula Parameters:
- **Rule Score Weight**: 50% (Max contribution: 50.0 points). Generated by 10 deterministic audit rules.
- **ML Anomaly Percentile Weight**: 30% (Max contribution: 30.0 points). Inverted Isolation Forest decision function rank (0.0 to 100.0%).
- **Statistical Risk Score Weight**: 20% (Max contribution: 20.0 points). Normalized max peer robust Z-score.
- **Risk Level Thresholds**:
  - `0.0 – 29.9`: **NORMAL**
  - `30.0 – 59.9`: **REVIEW**
  - `60.0 – 79.9`: **HIGH**
  - `80.0 – 100.0`: **CRITICAL**
- **Maximum Possible Score**: `100.0`
- **Math Exactness Check**: Evaluated across all 64,193 works. Difference between stored score and formula = **`0.0`** (100% exact).
